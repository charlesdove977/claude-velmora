# 05-glowbar (National)

URL: https://glowbar.com

## Hero
- Full-bleed close-up portrait, centered eyebrow (Face your skin with), bold geometric sans headline (30-Minute Custom Glowbar Facials), a dated promo sub line, one coral Book Now button. Promo bar above, coral Get $10 Off tab bottom-right.

## Nav
- Centered wordmark with links split either side (How it works, Membership, Locations | Shop, Careers), account, cart, outlined Book Now. Solid warm taupe, sticky.

## Booking CTA placement
- Nav Book Now, hero Book Now, membership, per-location booking, discount tab.

## Proof
- Customized for you section, membership, dermatologist-approved language. No review numbers on the hero.

## Pricing display
- Membership-first; single-service price on inner pages. $10 off tab.

## Motion
- Fades, three.js detected (bundled dependency, no visible 3D), background video present.

## Type + color
- BauPro (geometric grotesk) headings, DM Sans body, coral rgb(255,90,60) square buttons, warm taupe header and cream sections.

## Weaknesses
- Dated promo copy in the hero sub line. Discount tab and promo bar cheapen the frame. Careers in the primary nav. Coral reads retail, not clinical.

## Best element for Velmora
- Element: A single, plain-spoken time promise in the headline (30-minute facials) and a How it works nav item
- Why it works: Time-poor visitors book when they know exactly how long it takes and what happens.
- How Velmora adapts it: Velmora prints duration, discomfort, downtime, onset and longevity in a SpecStrip on every treatment page, and explains the visit in a four-step Method interlude on the home page.
