# 06-facefoundrie (National)

URL: https://facefoundrie.com

## Hero
- Triptych of three close-up portraits (diverse skin, hands on face) with a floating pill nav over it, big serif headline (Your Favorite Focused Facial Bar), one-line sub, white Book Now plus outlined Shop Now. A dark marquee (I really like your face) runs directly under the hero inside a rounded cream container.

## Nav
- Floating rounded pill header with inset margins: logo, Services dropdown, Facial Quiz, Locations, Franchise, Membership, Learn, cart, black Book Now. Stays floating on scroll.

## Booking CTA placement
- Nav Book Now, hero Book Now plus Shop Now, Facial Quiz in the primary nav, membership.

## Proof
- Marquee, locations count, membership, press. No review numbers on the hero.

## Pricing display
- Menu prices on the services pages; membership page. Not on home.

## Motion
- Marquee loop, rounded-container section transitions, hover states. No GSAP, Lenis or 3D detected.

## Type + color
- High-contrast editorial serif headings, Montserrat-style sans body, black plus cream palette, square buttons, cream background with rounded dark containers.

## Weaknesses
- Franchise link in the primary nav breaks the customer frame. Triptych photos fight the headline for attention. Cheeky marquee copy would not survive a medical context.

## Best element for Velmora
- Element: A quiz (Facial Quiz) promoted as a first-class primary nav item and hero-adjacent path
- Why it works: Gives the undecided visitor a low-commitment first action that ends in a booking.
- How Velmora adapts it: Velmora ships a six-question Treatment Match quiz linked from the hero (Find your treatment), the final CTA band and the footer, with results deep-linking into /book?treatment=.
