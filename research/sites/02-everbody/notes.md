# 02-everbody (National)

URL: https://everbody.com

## Hero
- Full-bleed treatment photo with big serif headline (The Gold Standard in Cosmetic Dermatology), sub line, green Book An Appointment plus text link. A promo modal and a Cherry financing slide-in both open on load.

## Nav
- Centered wordmark, mega-menu dropdowns (Treatments, Locations, Providers), green Book Now dropdown for location choice. Sticky white.

## Booking CTA placement
- Nav dropdown, hero button, per-treatment cards. Financing slide-in on every page.

## Proof
- 2,000 5-star reviews claim, providers page, philosophy page, before/after galleries.

## Pricing display
- Not on home; $300 promo thresholds. Membership in nav.

## Motion
- Subtle fades, background video (4 videos found). No GSAP, Lenis, 3D.

## Type + color
- Lora serif headings, Manrope Light body, deep teal rgb(0,84,83) square buttons, white background.

## Weaknesses
- Two popups on load (promo plus financing) bury the hero. Square buttons and teal read clinical. Heavy pages (77 images).

## Best element for Velmora
- Element: Financing explained inline with a three-icon strip (apply in minutes, low monthly, flexible terms)
- Why it works: Answers the cost objection before it is asked, in one glance.
- How Velmora adapts it: Velmora puts a financing line (Cherry, PatientFi) on every treatment pricing block and the pricing page, without the slide-in popup.
