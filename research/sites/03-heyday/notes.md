# 03-heyday (National)

URL: https://www.heydayskincare.com

## Hero
- Warm full-bleed facial photo, oversized geometric sans headline with an offer ($40+ off your first facial), single navy button, 3-slide carousel dots. Press logo marquee (Vogue, NYT, goop) directly under the hero. Email popup on load.

## Nav
- Utility bar (offer), then logo, dropdown links, Manage Appointments, search, cart, account, teal Book a Facial pill. Sticky.

## Booking CTA placement
- Nav pill, hero button, membership block (Become a Member), per-facial cards.

## Proof
- Press logo marquee, membership counts, skin analysis tool, esthetician pages.

## Pricing display
- Facial prices per studio; $20 to $40 offers. Membership in nav and body.

## Motion
- Logo marquee loop, carousel, gentle fades. video=yes. No 3D.

## Type + color
- Sunset Gothic (custom sans) throughout, navy plus cream palette, rectangular buttons, cream background.

## Weaknesses
- Discount-first hero cheapens the brand. Email popup on load. Long page (27 h2s) with repetitive blocks.

## Best element for Velmora
- Element: Press and proof marquee immediately under the hero, before any pitch
- Why it works: Borrowed authority in the first scroll, cheaply and without reading.
- How Velmora adapts it: Velmora's Trust strip sits directly under the hero with four hard numbers (years, treatments, rating, FDA-cleared devices) in the same slot, no third-party logos invented.
