# 01-skinspirit (National)

URL: https://www.skinspirit.com

## Hero
- Split hero: left copy on white, right full-bleed portrait of a laughing model. Script-style intro line over an all-caps Noto Serif Display headline. Single CTA (View Guide). Promo bar above nav. Cookie badge bottom-left.

## Nav
- Text links with dropdowns, dark green pill Book Now plus outlined Login and cart. Solid white, sticky. Mobile: logo, Book Now pill, hamburger.

## Booking CTA placement
- Nav pill, hero button, treatment-row Book a Consult, per-treatment pages. Booking opens their own scheduler.

## Proof
- Award-winning treatments band, Medical professionals block, three testimonial cards with city, no star counts on home.

## Pricing display
- Hidden on home; treatment pages list starting prices in some markets. Referral program ($100) shown.

## Motion
- Hero carousel (3 slides), simple fades. No GSAP, Lenis or 3D. video=yes (background clips).

## Type + color
- Noto Serif Display headings, Poppins body, dark green rgb(16,49,43) 31px pill buttons, white background.

## Weaknesses
- Promo bar plus cookie badge plus chat bubble compete for attention. Circle treatment tiles feel dated. Copy is generic (experts recommend).

## Best element for Velmora
- Element: Circle treatment tiles with a one-line label plus Book a Consult and See All Services side by side
- Why it works: Turns the treatment menu into an image-first, low-friction pick list right under the hero, with two paths (book or browse).
- How Velmora adapts it: Velmora's Signature Treatments grid uses tall editorial cards with from-$ pricing baked into the tile, and pairs the section with Book and See all rather than copying circle crops.
