# 07-beautify (Scottsdale)

URL: https://lovebeautify.com

## Hero
- Full-bleed smiling portrait with a script wordmark over the face, Medspa in Scottsdale, AZ in serif and italic, one teal pill CTA, then a proof line (Nearly 10,000 5-star reviews, 95.6% approval, 14.5+ years). Star-rating bar above the nav with phone.

## Nav
- Logo, seven text links (About, Day Spa, Medspa, Injectables, Specialty, Memberships, Gallery, Financing), teal pill Book Now. Sticky white.

## Booking CTA placement
- Top bar phone, nav pill, hero pill, section CTAs, Skin Analysis tool.

## Proof
- Review count in the top bar and hero, gallery page, memberships, financing page.

## Pricing display
- From $119 visible on service cards; $1,700+ packages. Membership page.

## Motion
- Fades, hover lifts. No video, no 3D.

## Type + color
- Ivyora Display serif plus system sans body, teal rgb(10,186,181) 50px pill, white background.

## Weaknesses
- Bright teal accent and stock smile read salon, not clinic. Script logo over the model's face hurts legibility. Busy nav.

## Best element for Velmora
- Element: Proof line beneath the hero CTA: review count, approval rate, years in business, in one row
- Why it works: Gives the local-trust answer (are they real, are they good, how long have they been here) before the fold ends.
- How Velmora adapts it: Velmora's four-stat Trust strip sits beneath the hero and repeats the medical-director credential; numbers are labeled demo.
