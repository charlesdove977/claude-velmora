## Positioning

What all ten do: a full-bleed portrait or treatment video hero, a Book Now pill in the nav, a treatment dropdown, some form of membership, and a promise about expertise. Six of ten interrupt the first visit with a promo bar, a discount popup or a financing slide-in. Eight of ten hide prices until an inner page. Palettes split cleanly into clinic (teal, dark green, navy, Helvetica or Poppins) and salon (coral, bright teal, script wordmarks over faces). Motion is limited to fades, carousels and background video; three of ten load three.js as a dependency without using it visibly.

What none of them do: publish a starting price on every treatment tile and page, show a spec strip (duration, discomfort, downtime, onset, longevity) before the pitch, give the medical director a real presence on the home page and a profile with schema, offer a concern-first entry point that filters the menu, provide aftercare guides and a searchable support hub, or use a single warm neutral system with one restrained metallic accent.

How Velmora wins: it takes the trust mechanics these sites prove (proof under the hero, financing inline, a quiz, a physician up front, membership math on the home page) and removes everything that undermines them (popups, discounts, hidden prices, clinical blue). It reads like a luxury hotel and explains like a clinic. The 3D liquid-glass hero and the scroll-scrubbed Method interlude are the memorable layer no competitor has, kept to two moments so the site stays fast, and served as posters to phones and reduced-motion users.
